Session setup checklist

  • Set Spotify quality to Lossless or Very High
  • Disable all system-level EQ and enhancement
  • Disable room-correction DSP
  • Level-match between sessions with an SPL meter
  • Allow the system 15–20 minutes to warm up

Glossary

PRAT
Pace, rhythm and timing. Whether music feels propulsive or sluggish.
Soundstage
The apparent width and depth of the performance between and beyond the speakers.
Sibilance
Exaggerated "s" and "t" sounds, often from a bright tweeter.
Level-matching
Setting two systems to the same loudness, typically within 0.5 dB, so the louder one doesn't win by default.
Sub-bass
Content below about 40 Hz, felt as much as heard.
